What we do

Design to production, under one roof

At R Howard | Carton-Pro we offer packaging solutions from design to production, handling runs from a few hundred to over a million units, including complex designs and builds.

We are committed to making sure that you get a product that truly suits your needs and we always work to your deadlines.

We print for the food and drink, beauty and personal care, pharmaceutical and household sectors, each with its own stock, finishing and compliance requirements.

Our offering

Lithographic printing

Up to six colours plus a varnish in a single press pass, on stocks from lightweight folding boxboard to heavy GC1.

In-house structural design

A full design service covering pack and carton structure, with plain samples produced for testing before a launch is committed.

Repro and artwork

Full print and repro facilities, so artwork can be created, corrected and proofed without leaving the building.

Die cutting and embossing

Full die cutting and embossing for complex builds, including the shapes most printers decline to quote for.

Window patching and gluing

Finished cartons patched, glued and delivered flat or made up, ready for your filling line.

Samples and shelf ready

Marketing samples, pre-production samples and fully finished shelf ready packaging, to the same specification as the live run.

Accreditations

Audited on food safety and on forestry

We hold FSSC 22000, the GFSI-recognized food safety scheme built on ISO 22000, and we print on FSC certified board so your pack can carry the FSC label.

Both certificates, with scope statements and licence codes, are issued for your supplier approval file on request.
