Step 3

Paste it into Outlook

Find the version of Outlook you actually use. If you are not sure, try the first one.

Outlook on the web, or new Outlook

outlook.office.com
  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. Open Settings, the gear at the top right.
  3. Go to Account, then Signatures.
  4. Name it R Howard and paste into the box with Ctrl+V.
  5. Under the box, set it for new messages and for replies and forwards.
  6. Save. Send yourself a test.

Outlook classic on Windows

desktop app
  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Outlook go to File, Options, Mail, Signatures.
  3. New, name it R Howard.
  4. Click into the edit box and paste with Ctrl+V.
  5. Top right, set New messages and Replies/forwards to your new signature.
  6. OK, then send yourself a test.

Outlook on a Mac

desktop app
  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. Outlook menu, Settings, Signatures.
  3. Press +, name it R Howard.
  4. Paste with Cmd+V, then close the window to save.
  5. Set it as the default for your account, for new messages and replies.
  6. Send yourself a test.

House rules

The signature only does its job if it looks the same coming from all of us.

  • Use the supplied lockup, nothing else. Do not paste in a logo from a document or a screenshot. The one in the signature is the approved artwork at the right size, and it carries alt text so it degrades to words when images are blocked.
  • Do not change the colours or fonts. Arial is used deliberately because every mail client on earth has it. Anything else silently falls back and looks wrong.
  • No quotes, no awareness ribbons, no green please-do-not-print lines. The sustainability line already says something true about what we sell.
  • Keep your job title as it appears on the org chart. Two people with the same role should read the same way.
  • Certification logos stay off email. BRCGS and FSC marks have artwork rules and belong on the website and on documents, where they can be reproduced properly.
  • Sending from a shared mailbox, for example enquiries or accounts, uses the same signature with the mailbox name in place of a person and no mobile.
