Our history

R Howard, established over 25 years

R Howard is a lithographic, carton printing company based in Peterborough, printing under the Carton-Pro name. For over 25 years R Howard has been a trusted and reliable supplier for customers, having grown from a firm foundation and building an established presence in the marketplace. We provide a nationwide service, and deliver into Europe, for businesses both large and small in a variety of different packaging sectors including food, beauty, pharmaceutical and household.

We're dedicated to excellent customer service and have a proud history of providing high quality folded carton packaging. Our reputation has been built on offering a personal and friendly service where bespoke solutions are found to suit individual project requirements.

FSSC 22000 ISO 22000 food safety

FSSC 22000 has been delivering impact on global food safety for 15 years. The Scheme provides a certification model that can be used in the food manufacturing industry and the related supply chain to ensure food safety standards and processes. FSSC 22000 is GFSI-recognized and follows the food chain category description as defined in ISO 22003-1:2022.

FSC Responsible forest management

FSC provide an internationally recognised standard for responsible forest management. These requirements are adapted at the national or sub-national level in order to reflect the diverse legal, social and geographical conditions of forests in different parts of the world.

By using FSC board we are supporting the development and future of the worlds forests.
