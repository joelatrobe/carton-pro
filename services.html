How we work

The process

01

Design consultation

We become involved even before your product launch, advising on pack and carton design. Our on-site design facilities allow us to design and manufacture product pack samples so that you can see exactly how your finished printed packaging will look.

We are committed to making sure that you get a product pack that truly suits your needs, no matter how difficult the design process. Our day to day business is manufacturing designs that are difficult to create and produce.

02

Plain samples for testing

Various plain carton samples are produced for testing, so the pack can be filled, dropped, stacked and shipped before a single colour goes on it.

03

Artwork, repro and printed samples

With full print and repro facilities we can create artwork and, if needed, manufacture small runs of fully finished printed samples for you. That covers marketing samples, pre-production and product launch samples, and fully finished carton or shelf ready packaging samples.

04

Litho production run

We are capable of printing in up to six colours plus a varnish, on runs from a few hundred cartons to over one million units, held to the specification signed off at sample stage.

05

Finishing and delivery

Embossing, full die cutting, window patching and gluing of finished product packs. We are used to working to tight deadlines, whilst still producing only the highest of quality products, then delivering anywhere in the United Kingdom or into Europe.

Tight deadlines, and only the highest quality.
